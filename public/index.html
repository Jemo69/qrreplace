QRReplace in 3 steps

  1. Design. Pick a preset on the left, or add a QR code, text and your logo. Drag things where you want them.
  2. Check. Turn on True output above the screen — that's exactly what the network sees. Green QR chip = phones can scan it.
  3. Go Live. Press Go Live. On another computer on the same Wi-Fi / network, open NDI Studio Monitor, OBS (NDI plugin) or vMix and choose your source name.

Tips: keep the QR bigger than a playing card on screen. Dark QR on a light card scans best. The top half can stay see-through so it overlays your live video.
